Remote Work Policy — Sample Document

Eligibility: Employees who have completed 6 months of service may apply for hybrid or fully remote work.

Core hours: Remote employees must be available 10:00 AM – 3:00 PM in their local timezone.

Equipment: The company provides one laptop and a one-time $300 home office stipend.

On-site days: Hybrid staff are expected on-site at least 2 days per week unless approved by their manager.
